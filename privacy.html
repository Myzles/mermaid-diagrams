Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Mermaid Diagrams for Confluence and Mermaid Diagrams for Jira ("the apps") run entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. We do not run servers of our own.

What the apps store

  • Confluence: the diagram text and its settings are saved inside the page by Confluence, like any other macro. A PNG picture of each diagram is saved as a page attachment so exports can show it.
  • Jira: diagram text and settings are saved as issue properties on the issue they belong to.

All of this stays in your Atlassian site, follows your site's permissions and data residency, and is deleted when you delete the page, attachment or issue.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps make no calls to any outside service, use no analytics or tracking, set no cookies and collect no personal data. We cannot see your diagrams, pages or issues.

Atlassian

Atlassian hosts the apps and handles billing and licensing. Their handling of data is described in the Atlassian Privacy Policy.
